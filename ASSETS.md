# Visual assets and source notes

## Original assets

The HexaBridge logo and corporate profile originate from the supplied 2026 corporate profile. The favicon is a simplified H in the existing brand colors.

## Generated artwork

`assets/fintech-devices-v2.webp` was generated using the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as WebP (1536 × 1024, approximately 88 KB). It is conceptual artwork, not actual SurePay hardware or evidence of a PNG deployment. The hero and kiosk card identify its conceptual status.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup / stylized-concept. Create a premium photorealistic 3D fintech product still life for the HexaBridge PNG corporate website. Landscape 3:2 composition. A beautifully crafted graphite black Android smart payment terminal with angled touchscreen and small numeric keypad, alongside a tall ivory self-service payment kiosk, with a floating rounded glass panel representing an electronic receipt. On the screen simple teal geometric payment interface, no readable words, no logos, no invented brand names, no QR code that could actually be scanned. Set on layered dark navy architectural plinths, deep navy #081e2a studio background, emerald and mint light ribbons connecting the devices, subtle teal rim lighting, premium industrial materials, atmospheric shadows, editorial product photography with restrained sophistication. Terminal in foreground at lower center, kiosk behind right, ample negative space on upper left. No people, no coins, no crypto, no floating dollar signs, no random network hexagons. This is a conceptual visualization, not a photograph of an actual SurePay product. Output image asset for web use.

Billing, electronic receipt, identity and collections diagrams are original HTML/CSS/SVG compositions, clearly marked as illustrative. They are not screenshots of partner software. The EOR receipt is marked as a demonstration and is not valid for transactions. Motion includes floating hero cards, signal bars, payment flow traces, a billing chart, an identity scan and section reveals. Pause motion and system reduced-motion preferences are supported.
